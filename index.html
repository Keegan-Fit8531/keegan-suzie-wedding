Stay the night

Cabin rooms at the camp

Invited guests can request an on-site cabin room through their personal RSVP link. Each invitation gets one cabin room. People on the same invitation share that room. Separate invitations get separate rooms.

  • Rooms have 6 to 8 single bunks (about 3 to 4 bunk beds)
  • Beds are camping mattresses only, so please bring your own blankets and pillows
  • Please be packed and out by 10:00 am the next morning, when our venue reservation ends
  • Rooms are reserved ahead of time. Without a request, one may not be available on the day
