Stay the night

Book a cabin when you RSVP

In the RSVP you can request an on-site cabin room at Hunua Falls Camp. If you ask to stay, we will reserve a room for you. Families each get their own separate room.

  • Cabin rooms have multiple bunks
  • Please bring your own blankets and pillows
  • Please be packed and out by 10:00 am the next morning, when our venue reservation ends
  • Rooms are reserved through the RSVP. If you do not book ahead, one may not be available on the day
